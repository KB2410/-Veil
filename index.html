The Privacy Model

Half light.
Half shadow.

Veil Feedback discloses only the facts a survey needs: a valid member participated, and that member has not responded before. The response remains private to its intended recipient. Midnight's selective-disclosure model makes this possible without trusted intermediaries.

👁 Visible on-chain
A valid credential participated
One-time nullifier (prevents duplicates)
Aggregate survey totals (count, average)
🔒 Never visible on-chain
Participant's wallet or identity
The secret credential itself
Individual feedback response or rating
Which member produced a particular answer

How It Works

Privacy-preserving flow

🔑

1. Local Proof

Your secret credential generates a ZK proof and survey-scoped nullifier entirely on your device.

🛡

2. Contract Verification

The Midnight contract verifies the proof, checks the nullifier hasn't been used, and rejects duplicates.

📊

3. Aggregate Only

Only the aggregate tally is updated on-chain. Your identity and response remain completely private.
